Safety guarantee - the number that matters most

Times a controller tried to cut green while a car was approaching: FIXED 0  |  SABZ 0
Times the safety rule blocked it and held green: FIXED 0  |  SABZ 0
Unsafe cutoffs actually executed: 0. The 70 m dilemma zone is checked every second; a switch under an approaching car is overridden, always.

How it works: cameras count cars per approach (120 m range) → the controller extends green for long queues, cuts it the instant a road clears, skips empty approaches, never starves any road → time-of-day plans learned from the map (schools, offices, mosques). Safety is a hard physics rule, not learned.
